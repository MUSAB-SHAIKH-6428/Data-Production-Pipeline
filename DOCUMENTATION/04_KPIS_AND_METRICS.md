# 04. KPIs & Business Metrics Dictionary

> **Focus:** Standardized business formulas, aggregation grains, and analytical definitions.

---

## 1. Sales & Revenue Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Formula | Grain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total (Gross) Revenue** | Total monetary value generated from customer orders including freight. | $\sum (\text{item\_price} + \text{freight\_value})$ | Day / Month / Category |
| **Net Revenue** | Realized sales value after deducting discounts, vouchers, and refunds. | $\sum (\text{item\_price}) - \sum (\text{discounts}) - \sum (\text{refunds})$ | Month / Seller |
| **Total Orders** | Total distinct confirmed purchase transactions. | $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{order\_id})$ | Day / Region / Channel |
| **Total Units Sold** | Total physical quantity of products fulfilled. | $\sum (\text{quantity})$ | Product / Category / Day |
| **Average Order Value (AOV)** | Average dollar expenditure per placed order. | $\frac{\text{Total Gross Revenue}}{\text{Total Orders}}$ | Month / Customer Segment |
| **Discount Amount** | Margin reduction resulting from promo codes and vouchers. | $\sum (\text{voucher\_value} + \text{coupon\_deduction})$ | Campaign / Period |

---

## 2. Customer & Retention Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Formula | Grain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Customer Count** | Total distinct active purchasers within a time period. | $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{customer\_unique\_id})$ | Month / Region |
| **New Customers** | Count of customers whose first order falls within the reporting period. | $\text{COUNT}(\text{customer\_unique\_id})$ where `first_order_date` $\in [\text{start}, \text{end}]$ | Month / Acquisition Channel |
| **Repeat Customer Rate** | Percentage of customer base with more than 1 completed purchase. | $\frac{\text{Customers with } > 1 \text{ Lifetime Orders}}{\text{Total Distinct Customers}} \times 100$ | Monthly Cohort |
| **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)** | Projected cumulative gross margin generated by a customer over their relationship. | $\text{AOV} \times \text{Purchase Frequency} \times \text{Avg Customer Lifespan}$ | Customer Segment |

---

## 3. Seller & Marketplace Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Formula | Grain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seller Revenue** | Total item revenue generated by a seller (excluding freight). | $\sum (\text{item\_price})$ grouped by `seller_id` | Seller / Month |
| **Seller Order Count** | Total order transactions fulfilled by a specific seller. | $\text{COUNT(DISTINCT } \text{order\_id})$ grouped by `seller_id` | Seller / Month |

---

## 4. Logistics & Operations Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Formula | Grain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **On-Time Delivery Rate** | Percentage of completed orders delivered on or before the estimated delivery date. | $\frac{\text{Orders with } \text{delivered\_date} \le \text{estimated\_date}}{\text{Total Delivered Orders}} \times 100$ | Courier / Region / Month |
| **Average Delivery Time** | Average duration (in days) from order placement to customer receipt. | $\text{AVG}(\text{delivered\_customer\_date} - \text{order\_purchase\_date})$ | Carrier / Route / Month |
| **Average Shipping Cost** | Average freight expense paid per order. | $\frac{\sum \text{freight\_value}}{\text{Total Orders}}$ | Origin Warehouse / State |
| **Cancellation Rate** | Percentage of placed orders canceled before delivery. | $\frac{\text{Orders with status 'canceled'}}{\text{Total Orders Placed}} \times 100$ | Day / Channel |
| **Return Rate** | Percentage of delivered orders returned for refund. | $\frac{\text{Returned Orders}}{\text{Total Delivered Orders}} \times 100$ | Product / Category / Seller |

---

## 5. Product & Catalog Metrics

| Metric | Business Definition | Formula | Grain |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Product Revenue** | Total revenue generated by an individual SKU. | $\sum (\text{quantity} \times \text{unit\_price})$ grouped by `product_id` | Product / Month |
| **Product Rating** | Average customer feedback review score (1 to 5 scale). | $\text{AVG}(\text{review\_score})$ | Product / Brand / Category |
